## Interview questions this project prepares for

1. Why should AI quality be a PM responsibility?
2. Why use a five-gate model instead of one overall score?
3. When should escalation count as success?
4. What would make you stop a launch even if adoption is high?
5. How would you design a controlled beta?
6. Which metric is the North Star and why?
7. How would you choose the confidence threshold?
8. What would you change after the first 1,000 customer sessions?

## What not to claim

Do not claim real customer traction, production AI accuracy, or revenue impact. The included scores are synthetic demo values. In an interview, explain the difference between a **product target**, a **prototype result**, and a **validated business outcome**.
