# 90-Second Demo Script

## 0–15 sec — Problem
“LaunchPilot AI is a product-management decision system for AI products. It answers a simple question: are we actually ready to launch?”

## 15–30 sec — Product brief
“Here is ResolveAI, a fictional support copilot. I can see the target user, problem, MVP, non-goals and North Star metric.”

## 30–50 sec — Five gates
“The PM then scores five launch gates: customer value, AI quality, trust and safety, operational readiness and business readiness. Critical AI or safety failures can block the launch.”

## 50–65 sec — Risk
“The risk register converts concerns into measurable exposure, mitigation and ownership. This prevents a risk document from becoming a static checklist.”

## 65–78 sec — Evaluation
“The evaluation workbench tests groundedness, task success, safe refusal, escalation and severe hallucinations.”

## 78–90 sec — Decision
“Finally, the product creates a Go, Iterate or No-Go decision memo. The PM gets a clear next action instead of another dashboard.”
