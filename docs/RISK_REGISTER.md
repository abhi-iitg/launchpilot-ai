## Risk scoring

Exposure = Likelihood × Impact.

- 1–3: Low
- 4–8: Medium
- 9–15: High
- 16–25: Critical

## PM principle

Risk should be tied to a product decision, mitigation owner and launch condition. A risk register is not useful if it only lists concerns without changing scope, rollout, monitoring or ownership.
