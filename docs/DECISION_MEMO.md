# Executive Launch Decision Memo — ResolveAI

## Decision
**ITERATE toward a controlled rollout.**

## Why
The demo scorecard clears the critical AI quality and trust gates, but several high risks remain. The right PM decision is to iterate on mitigations and then enter a controlled beta; real-world support volume, edge cases and customer trust cannot be validated by synthetic evaluation alone.

## Success criteria for Phase 1
- Maintain ≥90% groundedness
- Maintain <5% severe hallucination
- Achieve ≥20% ticket deflection on eligible intents
- Maintain ≥4.2/5 CSAT
- Keep escalation volume within support capacity

## Decision owner
Product Manager, with Engineering, Support Operations, Security and Customer Success as accountable partners.

## Next decision point
Review the launch scorecard after the controlled beta and either expand, iterate or rollback based on evidence.
