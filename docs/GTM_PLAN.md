# Go-to-Market & Rollout Plan

## Buyer
Head of Customer Support / Customer Experience leader at a mid-market SaaS company.

## Wedge
Start with repetitive, well-documented support questions where the downside of an incorrect answer is controllable.

## Rollout

### Phase 0 — Internal dogfood
- Support team only
- Approved knowledge base
- Daily quality review

### Phase 1 — Controlled beta
- 5–10% eligible customer traffic
- Human escalation enabled
- Weekly launch review

### Phase 2 — Expanded rollout
- 25–50% traffic if quality and safety guardrails remain stable
- Segment by low-risk intent types first

### Phase 3 — Scale
- Broader intent coverage
- Automated monitoring
- Quarterly evaluation refresh

## Positioning

> **Fast answers when the system knows; transparent escalation when it doesn't.**

## Primary adoption hypothesis

Customers will adopt AI support when the product is faster than human support for routine questions without forcing them to trade away trust.

## Launch channels

- Existing support portal
- Customer success outreach
- In-product education
- Admin enablement

## Kill / rollback criteria

- Critical privacy incident
- Severe hallucination above threshold
- Sustained CSAT decline
- Escalation volume exceeds operational capacity
